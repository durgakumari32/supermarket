Help & FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about placing bulk orders, delivery timelines, and payment options.

What is your delivery coverage radius and express turnaround?

We deliver to all residential and commercial addresses within a 25 km radius of Kanyakumari / Nagercoil. For regular home orders, express delivery takes between 45 to 90 minutes. For bulk wedding and HoReCa provisions, scheduled deliveries are executed as early as 4:00 AM.

How do wedding and event catering bulk orders work?

Simply send us your catering menu or required ingredients list. Our catering specialists will generate an optimized bill-of-quantities with wholesale pricing. We deliver directly to your mandapam/venue at your preferred time slot, and unopened non-perishable bags can be returned for full credit.

What are your minimum order values for free delivery?

Standard home grocery delivery is completely FREE on all orders above ₹499. For wholesale sacks and bulk hotel supplies, delivery is free on orders above ₹3,000.

Do you offer credit terms and GST invoices for businesses?

Yes, verified hotels, restaurants, and corporate clients are eligible for 15-day or 30-day billing cycles upon submission of basic business registration documents. Complete GST input-credit tax invoices are issued with every order.

What happens if any fruit or vegetable is not up to quality?

We offer a 100% No-Questions-Asked Replacement Guarantee. If any item is damaged, overripe, or unsatisfactory, simply notify us over WhatsApp within 4 hours, and our delivery executive will replace it or issue an instant refund.
