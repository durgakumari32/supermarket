Our Story

Rooted in Quality, Driven by Modern Service

Kumari Supermarket began in 2009 as a modest 800 sq.ft. grocery shop in the bustling heart of Kanyakumari district. Our founding principle was simple yet radical: Never sell anything you wouldn't proudly serve to your own family.

Over the past 15 years, our relentless focus on direct farm sourcing, strict quality sorting, and customer-first service has transformed us into a full-scale 12,000 sq.ft. modern supermarket and institutional supply enterprise.

Today, we are proud to serve more than 25,000 active household families, 150+ luxury hotels and restaurants, and hundreds of wedding organizers every single year with express cold-chain logistics.

Our Journey

Milestones in Our Evolution

A look back at how we grew from a small neighborhood market into an advanced supermarket supply hub.

2009

The Humble Beginning

Kumari Supermarket opened its doors with a single cash counter and 500 grocery lines in Kanyakumari.

2014

Bulk Wholesale & HoReCa Division

Launched dedicated supply contracts for local beach resorts, heritage hotels, and marriage catering groups.

2018

Cold-Chain Logistics Fleet

Invested in customized refrigerated temperature-controlled delivery vans to guarantee dairy & produce freshness.

2021

In-House Artisan Bakery & Live Counter

Added fresh daily bread, confectionery, artisanal cakes, and South Indian savories baked right on premises.

2024 - Present

Digital Express Delivery & Gifting Hub

Introduced 60-minute express doorstep delivery and custom corporate festival gift hampers across the entire district.
